Motion matching playground

Move with WASD (stick on touch screens), Shift = run, Q / E = turn on the spot. Camera: just move the mouse over the view to look around (no click; M toggles), wheel or pinch to zoom, drag one finger to orbit, arrow keys orbit, + / - zoom, C cycles camera modes, V resets, right-drag pans in free mode. Movement is relative to the camera. Everything you see is generated by this page: no downloads and no third-party data needed.
